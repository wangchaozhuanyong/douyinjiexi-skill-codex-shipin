# Copy Hook Scoring Rules

Score the hook before writing the full script.

## Required Inputs

- `internal/hook_variants.json` with at least 10 variants
- every variant has a first 3 seconds line
- every variant names a viewer pain or task
- every variant promises a visible result or proof

## Score Dimensions

- concrete AI object named: 1.5
- viewer pain in first 3 seconds: 1.5
- visible result promised: 1.5
- proof hint: 1.2
- save/reuse value: 1.0
- spoken Chinese rhythm: 1.0
- low hype and low empty talk: 1.3

Total: 10.

## Gate

The selected hook must score at least 8.5. If the best score is lower, rewrite the whole hook set.
